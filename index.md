# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 5, 2026

**Developer:** NashGames, United States. Contact: mr4nash4@gmail.com

This policy explains how the mobile game **Plink Party** (the "Game") handles information.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The Game doesn't collect, store on a server, sell or share personal information.
- **Account:** it doesn't ask you to create one.
- **Analytics:** it doesn't use analytics or advertising SDKs.
- **Advertising ID:** it doesn't access your device's advertising identifier.

## Data stored on your device

Your game progress (virtual cash, upgrades, settings) is saved only on your device, in the Game's private storage.
- We can't access it.
- You can delete it at any time in **Settings > Delete local save**, or by uninstalling the Game.

## App stores

Google Play and the Apple App Store may collect information when you download the Game, under their own privacy policies. We don't receive personal information from them about you.

## Children

The Game isn't directed at children under 13. It contains simulated gambling-style gameplay with virtual cash only. You can't win money or prizes, and virtual cash has no real-world value.

## Changes

If the Game changes how it handles information, for example by adding ads or online features in an update, we'll update this policy and the effective date before that update ships.
